Privacy

Your order details

This page explains what the NAZ Coffee website does with the information you enter when you order online.

What we collect

  • Your name and Mobile / Viber number, to prepare your order and contact you about it.
  • Your delivery area and address, only when you choose delivery.
  • Your order: items, options, order type, preferred time, payment method and any notes you write.
  • For Naz Rewards: your points balance is linked to your mobile number. Birthday and offer preferences are optional.

Where it goes

Orders are sent to the NAZ Coffee order board, and a backup copy goes to an order form managed by NAZ Coffee on Google Forms. Rewards points are kept by the NAZ Coffee rewards system. GCash payments are made in the GCash app and are not processed by this website.

On your device

Your cart, your mobile number and an unfinished checkout are saved in your browser, so you don't lose them if you leave the page. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand visits and orders, for example which drinks are added to carts. These services may set cookies.

Questions

To ask about or correct your details, mention it in your next order or contact NAZ Coffee directly.
